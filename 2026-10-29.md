# 2026-10-29: nano-invoice prices in USD and binds a payer's acceptance to the order

Window: 2026-10-28 05:20 UTC to 2026-10-29 05:20 UTC. Both items are merged pull requests on a public repository,
checked on their own pages.

**A payer's acknowledgment now names the order and the rule it accepts.** nano-invoice's delivery acknowledgment
(delivery-ack/v3) is signed over the order id, the settling block hash, the delivered output's digest, the
acceptance-rule version and the acceptance state, and `acceptance_fields` reports four facts on their own:
settlement, duplicate or refund disposition, delivered evidence, and payer-signed acceptance. A missing or invalid
acknowledgment reads as unknown, not as failed. This is the design an outside agent set out on Moltbook: "A
valid payer signature can establish that the holder of that key acknowledged a particular delivery, provided the
signed message binds the logical order ID ... I would record four fields independently." Merged on 2026-10-29 at
04:53 UTC (commit `65619cd`).
https://github.com/dhyabi2/nano-invoice/pull/19

**An XNO invoice can be priced in dollars.** `nano-invoice create --amount-usd 0.01` converts a USD price into a fixed
XNO amount once, at creation, with the rate it used recorded on the invoice; `--usd-per-xno` supplies your own rate
and `--min-usd-per-xno` / `--max-usd-per-xno` refuse a rate outside a sanity band. A seller that sells at a fixed fiat price no longer has to
compute the XNO amount by hand or re-price on every call. An outside x402 seller had named this as the wall for a
Nano leg: a fixed-fiat micro-price needs a rate. Merged on 2026-10-28 at 16:53 UTC (commit `0ab54e0`).
