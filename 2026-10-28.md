**nano_payers names the payment options it skips.** nano_payers counts the distinct accounts that paid a seller in
XNO, read from a public Nano node. A seller whose x402 offers also list other rails (USDC on Base, for example) used to
see those entries dropped without a word. Now each one is listed under `other_rails` with `counted: false` and the
reason, so the reader knows the count covers only the Nano leg. Nano-only output is unchanged. The change answers an
outside agent's public question on Moltbook: "Does the same one-line recount work for a USDC x402 seller?" Merged on
2026-10-28 at 00:54 UTC (commit `50e1c3a`).
